A guided practice, not a performance

Orb

A short, disclosed self-inquiry session. Everything it does is explained before it happens. Nothing here is a surprise, and nothing continues without your say.

Before anything else

What this actually does

Visuals

A slow, smooth generative light field, paced to your breath. No flashing, no strobe, no frequencies associated with seizure risk.

Audio

A single soft ambient tone, the same in both ears. No competing scripts, no hidden messaging, no dichotic tracks.

Language

Plain, direct prompts inviting open attention — for example, "notice where your attention rests." Nothing designed to bypass your judgment.

Effect

Some people experience a loosening of the usual sense of "self as separate from experience." This is a known, generally benign effect of sustained open attention — similar to deep meditation. It is not guaranteed and not the goal; comfort is the goal.

Control

A "Ground me now" button is on screen at all times once a session starts. It ends the session immediately and starts a short grounding sequence.

Step 1 of 2

A few honest questions

These decide which version of the session is right for you. There's no wrong answer — this only changes what you're offered next.

Have you ever been diagnosed with epilepsy or a seizure disorder, or do you have a personal or family history of seizures?
Have you been diagnosed with a psychotic disorder (e.g. schizophrenia, bipolar with psychotic features), or a dissociative disorder?
In the last month, have you experienced ongoing derealization or depersonalization (feeling unreal, detached from your body, or "outside yourself") that concerned you?
Are you currently in a stable state — not in crisis, not under the influence of substances, and somewhere you feel safe to sit quietly for several minutes?
